DOCTRINE · § 3

On Justification

☩ ORTHODOX

Theosis: justification as union with God, a synergistic process of deification.

— Gregory Palamas
✠ CATHOLIC

Infused grace through sacraments; faith working through love (Trent, Sess. VI).

— Council of Trent
✟ PROTESTANT

Forensic imputation: declared righteous by faith alone (sola fide).

— Martin Luther
KEY DISTINCTION

Process vs. declaration: is justification something done in the believer, or pronounced over the believer?

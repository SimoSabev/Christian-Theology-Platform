§ 1.1.1 · COSMOLOGICAL ARGUMENTS

The Kalam

A medieval Islamic-Christian inheritance, sharpened by William Lane Craig.

T he Kalam Cosmological Argument moves from the universe's beginning to its transcendent cause. Its modern formulation contains two premises and a conclusion that follows by the rule of inference modus ponens.

1. Whatever begins to exist has a cause.
2. The universe began to exist.
3. Therefore, the universe has a cause.
